## 5. Ordered implementation / investigation instructions

1. Schedule external work only from committed job intents with scoped identity and idempotency keys.
2. Provide start/observe/complete/reconcile hooks for verifier, Git publication, maintenance and update workers without encoding their product policy in transport.
3. Record job ownership/heartbeat/result independently from task execution ownership.
4. Reconcile crash-after-side-effect through artifact/commit/process readback; do not rerun a non-idempotent effect blindly.
5. Bound job concurrency, output retention and cancellation acknowledgement.
6. Ensure recovery jobs cannot grant a new work fence or overwrite a committed accepted outcome by themselves.
