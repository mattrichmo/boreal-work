## 5. Ordered implementation / investigation instructions

1. Implement explicit role/delegation changes with reason, expected authority revision, actor identity and durable operation outcome.
2. Recheck authority at transaction time so revocation races cannot authorize a later mutation on a cached role.
3. Define what credential revocation does to active executions, pending operations and future review/publication actions under the approved policy.
4. Preserve historical attribution and already-committed decisions; do not delete principals referenced by audit/history.
5. Require a safe last-operator recovery procedure rather than accidentally removing all administrative access.
6. Provide readback and a minimally disclosed security audit of accepted and rejected changes.
