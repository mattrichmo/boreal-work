## Work

1. Reconcile existing due, not-before, planned-window, Intake revisit and Global follow-up semantics. Do not conflate business dates with attempt lease/renewal/hard deadlines or duplicate Global scheduling engines.
2. Add a typed project external-wait record or supported equivalent: blocked work, reason/category, accountable person/role or external service reference, expected decision/output, follow-up/revisit time, state and attributable resolution. Resolve through authorized application operations; prose or a timer firing is not approval.
3. Required waits block only their dependents. Derive ready/blocked/overdue/revisit views with project civil-time context, timezone changes and date-only rules. Optional dates remain optional. Deferral does not fake completion; missed follow-up does not erase an obligation.
4. Expose schedule/wait operations through the registered typed boundary. Follow-up attention is a local work obligation, not implicit permission to send email, create a bank account or spend money.
5. Cover DST gaps/folds, midnight, overdue carry-over, changed due dates, waiting on an unavailable provider, late/repeated resolution, actor permissions and cancellation. Preserve history in backup/import and clearly distinguish resolved wait from completed work.
